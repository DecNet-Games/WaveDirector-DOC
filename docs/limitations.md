# Limitations — what this director is not

Stated plainly, so you can judge the fit before you buy:

- **Not a behaviour-tree framework.** It directs encounters — which enemies, how many, when, at what pressure. What an individual enemy decides moment-to-moment is outside its scope.
- **No enemy content.** No 3D model packs, no sprite packs, no tileset art. Demo-scene art is minimal, clearly labelled placeholder, and reskinnable.
- **No custom rendering.** No custom shaders, no Shader Graph systems, no VFX packs.
- **Single-player only.** No networking, no shared-wave server, no co-op wave sync.
- **The pacing graph shows pacing, not fun.** Telemetry tells you where your game gets easy or brutal; what to do about it remains your design judgement — the director makes that judgement evidence-based, not automatic.
- **One product, one edition.** There is no second tier or add-on module; what is documented here is what ships.
