# Presets & Demo Scenes

## Three presets

One per genre, each a complete configuration — curve, budget costs, composition rules, spawn mode and demo tuning — stored as data files in the package. Tower defense and survivors-like are the two genres the product is named for; the third preset serves a third genre the director genuinely supports, chosen and frozen in the verification record. The three differ measurably in curve shape and composition rules — they are not relabels of one another.

## Three demo scenes

One playable scene per preset:

- A **tower-defense scene** with paths, towers and directed waves.
- A **survivors-like scene** with continuous pressure spawning.
- A **third scene** matching the third preset.

Each scene is a playable game slice — a win condition, a lose condition, player interaction — not a bare spawner showcase. Each scene's waves are driven by its preset, and each playthrough records telemetry you can open in the viewer. All three scenes use only package-included content: no external dependency, no missing-reference errors on import.
